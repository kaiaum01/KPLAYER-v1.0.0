# Contributing

Beta feedback is welcome. Use an issue to report the exact application version, R36S variant, firmware, symptoms, media extension/codec/resolution and relevant log output. Confirm whether B confirms, A goes back and SELECT opens Settings on your device.

For code changes, keep the 640 × 480 layout readable and update all three interface languages. Preserve the independent music queue, runtime preferences and third-party notices. Run checks appropriate to the change; instructions are in [docs/BUILD.md](docs/BUILD.md).

Do not commit personal media, runtime configuration, logs, build toolchains or temporary output. The public image fixtures are intentionally original and illustrative. Keep new dependencies documented and include their required licenses.

## Português

Relate versão, aparelho, firmware, sintoma, codec/resolução da mídia e trechos do log. Confirme também o mapeamento A/B no seu console.

Nas alterações, preserve a legibilidade em 640 × 480, atualize PT-BR/EN/ES, mantenha a fila independente e os avisos de terceiros. Confira os testes relevantes descritos em [docs/BUILD.md](docs/BUILD.md). Evite incluir mídias pessoais, configurações e arquivos temporários.
